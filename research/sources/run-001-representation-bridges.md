# Sources for the representation arguments

Accessed October 28, 2026. Purpose: support the precise bridges in the [submitted finding](../findings/run-001-representation-obstruction.md). This record is not a novelty search or a reconstruction of the full cited literature.

## Frobenius trace and determinant

Andrew Sutherland, MIT 18.783, Fall 2025, Lecture 25 dated December 11, 2025, section 25.3, printed pages 3 and 4. [Official course PDF](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.783/2025/LectureNotes25.pdf).

The full relevant passage was inspected. For an elliptic curve over Q, at a good prime q distinct from the torsion prime ell, the mod ell action of arithmetic Frobenius has trace congruent to `q+1-#E(F_q)` and determinant congruent to q. These are the only external facts from this source used in the control curve argument. The no invariant line inference is supplied explicitly in the finding. The lecture does not by itself prove BSD for the control.

## Quadratic twist of the torsion representation

John E. Cremona and Nuno Freitas, *Global methods for the symplectic type of congruences between elliptic curves*, Revista Matematica Iberoamericana 38 (2022), issue 1, pages 1 to 32, DOI 10.4171/RMI/1269. [Publisher PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/38965).

Printed page 8, Lemma 2.2(2), equation (2.5), identifies the quadratic twist representation with the original representation tensored by the quadratic character. The surrounding derivation and statement were inspected. Scalar twisting preserves invariant subspaces; the finding also gives an explicit isomorphism argument. The proposition does not require the invariant line to be pointwise rational after twisting.
