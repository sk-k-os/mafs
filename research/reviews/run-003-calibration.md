## Failure tests fixed before final candidates

| Test | Required distinction |
| :--- | :--- |
| A statement says rank two | Specify Mordell Weil, analytic or Selmer rank. A Selmer upper bound and points on the equation do not alone give equality. |
| Two rational points are presented | Verify independence modulo torsion, or retain the exact theorem that supplies it. |
| A 2 descent matrix has nullity two | Check whether rational 2 torsion has already been removed. Record the exact Selmer convention. |
| Sha[2] vanishes | This excludes every nonzero element of 2 power order; it neither excludes odd torsion nor proves full Sha finite. |
| A functional equation has positive sign | The analytic order is even, not necessarily zero or two. A numerical central value needs an error certificate. |
| A prime qualified family is constructed | A theorem for every admissible parameter does not prove infinitely many parameters satisfy simultaneous primality. |
| A twist preserves residual irreducibility | Scalar twisting preserves invariant subspaces; it does not preserve rational generators of torsion lines. |
| A theorem assumes semistability | Recheck primes dividing the twist discriminant, including 2 and 3. Ramified twists can acquire additive reduction. |
| A theorem applies at an ordinary prime | Confirm good ordinary or multiplicative reduction as required. Do not move an ordinary conclusion to a supersingular prime. |
| The selected prime is 3 | Retain every extra supersingular trace condition and any stronger local hypothesis in the exact theorem used. |
| Residual ramification is required | A multiplicative prime alone does not suffice. Verify the residual inertia action, including divisibility of the minimal discriminant valuation. |
| A recent source removes a hypothesis | Inspect the current version, section assumptions and theorem statement; do not infer the removed hypothesis from an abstract alone. |
| A prime part of BSD is supplied | Preserve its prime, rank restriction, torsion denominator and local factors. Do not call it the full leading coefficient formula. |
| No applicable theorem is found | Report the bounded source coverage, not a claim that no theorem exists. |

## Early observations

The referee independently inspected Reinholz, Spearman and Yang, *On Congruent Numbers with Three Prime Factors*, Integers 11 (2011), A20, Theorem 1 and its proof. The theorem concerns exact Mordell Weil rank two. Its family infinitude is explicitly conjectural. Remark 5 describes an omitted descent for the uniform specialization lower bound. These are source and replay boundaries, not a rejection of the published theorem. [Primary paper](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/l20/l20.pdf)

The referee independently inspected the retained published JSW Theorem 7.2.1 on printed page 422. Part (ii) genuinely permits curves that are not globally semistable, but requires exact analytic rank zero, the specified ordinary or multiplicative condition and a multiplicative prime with residual ramification. This differs from the main analytic rank one theorem. [Published paper](https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cjm/2017/0005/0003/CJM-2017-0005-0003-a002.pdf)

These observations were communicated before any final verdict. Final route verdicts will bind exact candidate revisions in the [referee report](run-003-referee.md). No score or benchmark clearance is assigned.
