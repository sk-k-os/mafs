## What the discussion changed

The CM route is more specific than a generic literature audit: the inspected original theorem identifies an exact missing valuation. The symbolic screen also reveals an obstacle. The proposed trace field has degree 25,724,160, so constructing its full defining polynomial is rejected as a cheap next step. That degree is not a lower bound on every possible algorithm. The two required modular traces may admit a smaller computation; no such method is currently established in these records. This is the immediate feasibility question.

The broader route identifies a different mathematical witness. A nonzero generalized Kato class could constrain the rational Selmer dimension. That supplies primary finiteness, not the exact order. A theorem that already assumes primary Sha finiteness cannot be used to prove that premise. Similarly, a derived point membership criterion for a different class of curves needs an independent construction of the membership certificate; restating an equivalence is not a proof.

The non CM derived point framework remains a potential comparison of mechanisms. It excludes the current CM curve under its standing hypotheses. Global semistability is not to be imposed indiscriminately on every ordinary case; the broader source note preserves the supersingular and ordinary distinctions. A contrasting curve is useful only after the property being tested and all relevant hypotheses are specified. No new curve is selected here.

## The question that connects local success to a solution

An independently constructed nonzero integer M annihilating Sha, together with finite M Selmer, would give full finiteness. Naming M merely reformulates the target unless a separate arithmetic construction actually yields it. Even finiteness of every primary component, with square orders and nondegenerate alternating pairings, does not imply a finite group: the direct sum over primes of (Z/pZ)^2 supplies an elementary countermodel. This is not claimed to occur as an elliptic curve's Sha.

Full finiteness still leaves the BSD coefficient identity. Rational Selmer dimensions discard finite torsion, and valuations at finitely many primes do not determine a global rational quantity. A future route needs an independently justified common normalization, a saturated regulator, local correction factors, and control at all primes. The present rank two mechanism also needs a further extension before addressing arbitrary rank and curves without CM. These are explicit missing links, not consequences of the example.
